Most focus apps nag you. Focal changes what is on the screen, so the break is the path of least resistance.

Blur, not a popup

After your work block the screen eases into a blur in five steps. Time only counts while you are using the keyboard or mouse, and a short pause of up to two minutes still counts as working.
