What makes it different for health

An AI front desk that knows its limits

It answers new inquiries in seconds, day or night, on the channels patients actually use. It never gives clinical advice, it states plainly that it is not a clinician, and anything that reads as a crisis is escalated to 988 and 911 immediately.

We do not touch patient records

No charts, no histories, no intake content, no screening instruments. The front door is ours, the clinical record stays yours. Session analytics run with all page text masked by default, so nothing a visitor types is ever recorded.

Insurance and pricing answered up front

Cost and coverage are the first two questions every patient has and the two most practices bury. Published clearly, they also give Google and AI assistants something real to cite when someone asks who takes their plan.

Reviews, handled quietly

In behavioral health a review is a big ask, so the request has to be gentle and correctly timed. It runs automatically after a visit and it is the single fastest way to outrank the practice with a similar name that is currently sitting above you.

Built to be found by AI, not just Google

One of our clients got a patient who found her by asking ChatGPT who the best provider near her was. Structured services, published pricing and real reviews are what make a practice legible to an assistant that is answering that question.

One invoice, not five logins

Site, hosting, CRM, booking path, reviews and reporting under one system and one bill, usually for less than those tools cost stacked separately. No ad spend markup, ever.

Kansas City based, practices nationwide

We are based in Kansas City, Missouri and our current health clients are Missouri telehealth practices, including St. Louis. None of this depends on being local. The whole system runs remotely, onboarding happens over video, and a practice in another state is set up exactly the same way. If you are a KCMO or St. Louis practice, you also get the local visibility work: Google Business Profile, map presence and the directory listings that decide who shows up for "psychiatrist near me".
