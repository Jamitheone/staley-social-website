Common questions

Where is my prospect list saved?

On your device only. Open a prospect to edit it or delete it, or use Clear all data in the About screen to remove everything. Deleting the app deletes the list too, and we cannot see or recover it.

Does the app need an account, internet or access to my contacts?

No to all three. It works fully offline, has no sign-in, and never reads your phone's contacts. Tapping a phone number or email address hands it to your Phone or Mail app, and the app itself asks for no permissions.

Can I move my list to a new phone or back it up?

Not in this version. There is no cloud backup and no export, so a new phone starts empty. If you restore your phone from a full device backup, your list may come back with it, but the app does not manage that.

What does Load sample data do?

It adds six clearly fake prospects, each marked (sample), so you can see how the app works. Remove sample rows or Clear all data in the About screen takes them out again, and your own prospects are never touched by Remove sample rows.
