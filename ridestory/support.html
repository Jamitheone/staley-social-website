Common questions

Which files does it open?

GPX files whose track points carry timestamps. Pick one in the Import GPX button. A file without times cannot give speeds, so the app tells you it could not read it.

Is my ride uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read on your phone, the story is built on your phone, and nothing leaves the device. The app has no network code at all.

Does the story invent facts?

No. The story is a template filled with numbers from your track, and a guard rejects any number that is not in your data.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-in and no permissions.
