Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Ride Story (the "app") is an app that turns a recorded GPX ride into a ride card and a short written story. It is made by The Staley Social LLC.

Short version: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network requests. We cannot see anything you type into it.

What stays on your device

  • The GPX file you pick is read on your device to build the card and story. The app does not keep a copy and does not send it anywhere

It is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address or account. The app has no sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. The app asks for no permissions.
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics.
  • No data is shared with, sold to or sent to any third party, because no data is collected.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it does not connect to any server.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Good to know

Ride Story is an unofficial tool for hobby use. Speeds and distances come from your own track file and can be off if the recording was. It is not a safety or navigation aid.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
