Practice Planner Support

Need help with the app? Email thestaleysocial@gmail.com with your phone model, app version and what happened, and a person answers.

Common questions

Where are my bookings saved?

On your device only. There is no cloud copy and no backup, so deleting the app or using Clear all data in the About tab deletes everything, and a new phone starts empty.

What should I type as a booking label?

A first name, initials or a short code, up to 16 characters. The app refuses things that look like an email, a phone number or a date of birth. Please do not enter health information.

Can it send reminders or sync to a calendar?

Not in this version. The app works fully offline, makes no network requests, sends no notifications and asks for no permissions. Tell us if reminders would help.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. There is no sign-in and nothing to set up online. Open the app, add a provider or room, set the weekly hours and start booking.
