What a 90-day marketing guarantee actually means

November 23, 2026

Many small business owners in Kansas City are lured in by the promise of a 90-day marketing guarantee, but what does it really mean? If your agency promises a guarantee, it's essential to understand what you're getting into.

In the best-case scenario, a 90-day marketing guarantee means that your agency has a clear plan in place to drive real results for your business. This plan should be based on sound marketing principles and should have measurable goals. Your agency should have a thorough understanding of your target audience, your competitors, and the market as a whole.

However, in many cases, a 90-day marketing guarantee is little more than a smoke screen. It can be a way for agencies to lock in long-term contracts without delivering tangible results. Be wary of agencies that promise the moon in the first 90 days. Any legitimate agency should be able to demonstrate a clear plan and strategy for driving real results.

So, what should you expect from a 90-day marketing guarantee? At the very least, you should expect a clear plan of action that is tailored to your business's unique needs. This plan should include specific marketing channels, tactics, and metrics for measuring success. Your agency should be transparent about how they will be tracking progress and what they will do if the results aren't meeting expectations.

To hold your agency accountable, it's essential to have a clear understanding of what success looks like for your business. This means setting specific, measurable goals that align with your overall business objectives. You should also have regular check-ins with your agency to review progress and make adjustments as needed.

Finally, be wary of agencies that are overly vague about their guarantee. Any legitimate agency should be able to clearly explain what they will do, and what you can expect, if the results aren't meeting expectations. Don't be afraid to ask questions and push for clarity. Your business's success is worth it.
