How much should a small business in Kansas City spend on Google Ads

August 24, 2026

If you're allocating $500 per month to Google Ads, it's likely not getting the return on investment you expect. As a small business owner in Kansas City, you want to make sure your online advertising budget is working hard for you. But with so many variables at play, it can be tough to determine the right amount to spend.

The truth is, there's no one-size-fits-all answer to this question. The ideal Google Ads budget for your business will depend on a range of factors, including your industry, target audience, competition, and marketing goals. However, we can give you some general guidelines to help you get started.

First, consider the average cost-per-click (CPC) in your industry. According to Google's own data, the average CPC for searches in Kansas City is around $1.50. However, this can vary widely depending on the specific keywords and industries you're targeting. For example, if you're a dentist in Overland Park, you may find that your CPCs are much higher than those of a small business owner in Wyandotte County.

Next, think about the volume of searches you want to reach with your ads. Are you looking to drive traffic to your website, generate leads, or make sales? The more targeted and relevant your ads are, the lower your CPCs will be. But if you're casting a wide net, you may need to spend more to get the same number of clicks.

As a general rule of thumb, consider allocating 10-20% of your total online advertising budget to Google Ads. This can help ensure that you're getting a good return on investment while also giving you room to experiment and adjust your strategy as needed.

For example, if your total online marketing budget is $5,000 per month, you might allocate $500-$1,000 to Google Ads. Of course, this will depend on your specific business needs and goals.

To give you a better sense of what works in Kansas City, here are some real-life examples of successful Google Ads campaigns from our agency:

* A local restaurant spent $2,000 per month on Google Ads and saw a 25% increase in website traffic and a 15% boost in sales. * An IT services company allocated $1,500 per month to Google Ads and generated an average of 20 new leads per week. * A small retail business spent $750 per month on Google Ads and increased its online sales by 30%.

These are just a few examples, but they demonstrate the potential for Google Ads to drive real results for Kansas City small businesses. By understanding your industry, target audience, and marketing goals, you can create a Google Ads strategy that works for you – not against you.

Now it's time to put these principles into action. Take a closer look at your current Google Ads budget and see if there are opportunities to optimize and improve your results. With the right approach, you can get more out of your online advertising dollars and achieve real success for your business.
