How much should a small business in Kansas City spend on Google Ads

August 24, 2026

When you're deciding how much to spend on Google Ads, you're essentially trying to figure out how much of your marketing budget to allocate to this one channel. You want to make sure you're getting a good return on your investment, but you don't want to overspend either.

To start, you should think about what you're trying to achieve with your Google Ads campaign. Are you looking to drive more traffic to your website, generate leads, or get more phone calls? Different goals will require different budget allocations.

You'll also want to consider your target audience and how competitive your industry is. If you're in a highly competitive industry, you may need to spend more to get your ads seen. On the other hand, if you're in a niche industry with less competition, you may be able to get away with spending less.

In terms of setting an actual budget, you'll want to start by setting a daily budget for your campaign. This will help you control how much you're spending each day and prevent you from overspending. You can always adjust your budget later if you find that you're not getting the results you want.

It's also important to think about the cost-per-click (CPC) for your ads. This is the amount you'll pay each time someone clicks on your ad. You'll want to make sure you're bidding enough to get your ads seen, but not so much that you're overspending.

Ultimately, the key to setting a successful Google Ads budget is to start small and adjust as you go. You don't want to throw a lot of money at a campaign that may not be working, so it's better to start with a smaller budget and gradually increase it if you find that it's effective.

By taking a thoughtful and intentional approach to setting your Google Ads budget, you can help ensure that you're getting the most out of your marketing dollars and driving real results for your small business in Kansas City.

You should also consider monitoring your campaign's performance regularly and making adjustments as needed. This will help you stay on track and make sure you're getting the best possible return on your investment.

By following these steps and being mindful of your budget, you can create a successful Google Ads campaign that helps you achieve your marketing goals and grow your small business in Kansas City.
