How much should a small business in Kansas City spend on Google Ads

October 26, 2026

You're spending $2,000 a month on Google Ads, but not getting the return on investment you expected. You've tried adjusting keywords, ad copy, and targeting, but the numbers just aren't adding up. It's time to rethink your approach and set a budget that actually drives sales and growth for your business.

The first step is to understand that Google Ads is a performance-based marketing platform. You only pay when someone clicks on your ad, which means every dollar counts. But to make the most of your budget, you need to set realistic goals and expectations. A good starting point is to consider your ad spend as a percentage of your total marketing budget.

Most small businesses allocate 5-10% of their marketing budget to Google Ads. This can vary depending on your industry, target audience, and specific marketing goals. For example, if your total marketing budget is $10,000 a month, allocating 5-10% of that to Google Ads would put you in the range of $500 to $1,000 per month.

However, this is just a rough guideline. The key is to focus on return on investment (ROI). Instead of setting a fixed budget, try to set a minimum ROI threshold that you're comfortable with. For example, if you want to generate $1,000 in sales revenue for every $500 spent on Google Ads, that's a 200% ROI.

To achieve this, you'll need to optimize your ad campaigns to target the right audience, increase ad relevance, and improve conversion rates. This means continually monitoring your ad performance, testing new ad copy and targeting options, and making adjustments as needed.

Ultimately, setting a realistic budget for Google Ads requires a combination of understanding the platform, setting clear goals, and continually optimizing your campaigns. By following these steps and focusing on ROI, you can create a budget that drives actual sales and growth for your Kansas City small business.
