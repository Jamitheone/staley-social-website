Signs your marketing agency isn't tracking conversions correctly

November 2, 2026

You're spending thousands of dollars on marketing every month, but are you really getting the results you need? If you're like many business owners in Kansas City, you're probably relying on your marketing agency to track the effectiveness of their campaigns and give you accurate numbers on conversions. But what if we told you that many agencies aren't doing it right?

One in five businesses has reported issues with their marketing agency's tracking and measurement methods. This can lead to a whole host of problems, including wasted budget, missed opportunities, and a lack of clarity on what's working and what's not.

So, what are the signs that your marketing agency isn't tracking conversions correctly? Here are three common mistakes to watch out for:

First, are they only tracking the most obvious metrics? Many agencies focus on website traffic and social media engagement, but these numbers don't always translate to real-world conversions. Make sure your agency is tracking metrics like phone calls, form submissions, and in-store sales.

Second, are they using the right tools? Just because an agency has a shiny new tracking system doesn't mean it's the right one for your business. Look for an agency that has experience with a variety of tracking tools and can recommend the best one for your specific needs.

Third, are they transparent about their methods? A good marketing agency should be able to explain exactly how they're tracking conversions and what numbers they're looking at. If they can't or won't provide clear answers, it may be time to look elsewhere.

If you're unsure about your marketing agency's tracking methods, it's time to take a closer look. Here are some steps you can take to get a better understanding of what's going on:

Ask your agency to walk you through their tracking process from start to finish. What metrics are they using, and how are they collecting the data? This will give you a better understanding of what's happening behind the scenes.

Request to see the actual tracking data. Don't just take your agency's word for it – look at the numbers for yourself. This will help you spot any discrepancies or areas where the agency might be fudging the numbers.

Have a discussion with your agency about what numbers are most important to you. Don't be afraid to push back if you don't like the metrics they're using or if you think they're not giving you the whole picture.

By following these steps and being proactive about your tracking, you can avoid common mistakes and get a clear picture of what's working and what's not.
