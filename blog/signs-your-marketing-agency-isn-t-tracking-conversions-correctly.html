Signs your marketing agency isn't tracking conversions correctly

July 14, 2026

As a small business owner in Kansas City, you're likely investing a significant amount of time and money into your marketing efforts. You're probably working with a marketing agency to help you reach new customers and drive sales. But are you getting the most out of your marketing budget? One way to find out is to take a close look at how your agency is tracking conversions.

If you're not seeing the return on investment you expect, it may be because your agency isn't tracking conversions correctly. Conversions are the actions you want your customers to take, such as filling out a form, making a purchase, or signing up for a newsletter. If your agency isn't tracking these conversions accurately, you won't have a clear picture of what's working and what's not. You'll be making decisions based on incomplete or inaccurate data, which can lead to wasted time and money.

So how can you tell if your marketing agency isn't tracking conversions correctly? One sign is if they're not using a clear and consistent definition of a conversion. If they're counting every phone call as a conversion, but not every form submission, you'll get a skewed view of your marketing performance. Another sign is if they're not tracking conversions across all of your marketing channels. If they're only looking at website traffic, but not at social media engagement or email opens, you'll be missing out on valuable insights.

You should also be wary if your agency is relying too heavily on vanity metrics, such as likes and followers. While these metrics can be useful for gauging engagement, they don't necessarily translate to real-world conversions. If your agency is focusing too much on these metrics, they may be missing the bigger picture. You want an agency that can tie their marketing efforts back to real-world results, such as sales, revenue, and customer acquisition.

To get a better handle on your marketing performance, you should ask your agency to provide you with regular, detailed reports on your conversions. These reports should include metrics such as conversion rate, cost per conversion, and return on ad spend. If your agency can't provide you with this information, or if they seem evasive or unclear when you ask for it, it may be time to look for a new agency. You deserve to know exactly how your marketing budget is being spent, and what kind of results you're getting.

In Kansas City, there are many marketing agencies to choose from, and not all of them are created equal. When you're evaluating an agency, make sure to ask tough questions about how they track conversions. Do they use a clear and consistent definition of a conversion? Do they track conversions across all of your marketing channels? Can they provide you with regular, detailed reports on your conversions? If you're not getting the answers you want, it may be time to look elsewhere. Your marketing budget is too valuable to waste on an agency that isn't delivering real results.
