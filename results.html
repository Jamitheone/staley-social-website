Case Study 01, Sports Marketing

Kansas City Comets

PROFESSIONAL INDOOR SOCCER, MASL, KANSAS CITY, MO

The KC Comets came to us with a Google Ads account pointing traffic to the wrong arena, no conversion tracking in place, and a website that required 3 clicks just to buy a ticket. They had ad budget, but no infrastructure to make it work.

We started by stopping the bleeding: rerouted all traffic to Cable Dahmer Arena, set up proper geofencing in a 3-tier radius system, installed Microsoft Clarity for session recording and heatmap analysis, and rebuilt the Google Ads account from scratch.

The website prototype we built in one hour, shown side-by-side with their existing site, reduced the ticket purchase path from 3 clicks to 1. The result in month 1: 20% more ticket sales.

Case Study 02, Commercial Real Estate

Office Brokers of SWFL

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE, SOUTHWEST FLORIDA

Jeff Moore reached out independently after seeing our work. His firm needed Google Ads, not a generic template, but a precision campaign targeting commercial tenants and buyers in Southwest Florida with zero wasted spend.

We built the account from scratch: keyword research, audience segmentation, conversion tracking for phone calls and form submissions, and a campaign structure designed around commercial real estate search behavior.

Within 7 days of launch, the campaign generated 14+ qualified leads. The engagement runs at $700/month with 100% margin, zero overhead, all value.
