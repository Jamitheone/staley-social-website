Rider Lab KC Support

Need help with the app? Email thestaleysocial@gmail.com with your handle and what happened, and a person on the crew answers. Include your phone model and app version if something is broken.

Common questions

I am not on the live map.

Check that location permission is on, you are not in Ghost mode, and you are not inside a privacy zone. The map drops anyone who has not sent a position in a few minutes.

My lean angle reads wrong.

Open the You tab and run Calibrate lean sensor once per phone mount: sit the bike upright, set zero, then lean left to set direction. The Flip lean direction switch fixes a mirrored sign.

My ride did not save.

Rides under 30 seconds or with too few GPS points are not saved. With no signal at the end of a ride the app queues the save and sends it when you are back online.
