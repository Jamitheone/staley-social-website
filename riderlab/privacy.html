Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Rider Lab KC (the "app") is a free crew app for motorcycle riders in the Kansas City area: a live crew map, GPS ride tracking, lean-angle readings, routes, a garage and a feed. It is made by The Staley Social LLC ("we", "us"). This page says what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Short version: we collect what the features need, we do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and the iPhone and Android apps contain no advertising or analytics tools.

What we collect and why

DataWhat it is and why
AccountYour email address and a sign-in credential. You sign in with email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password. From Apple or Google we receive your email and, if you allow it, your name.
ProfileName, rider handle, bike, city, years riding and riding styles that you type in. Used to show you on the crew list, garage and leaderboard.
Precise locationOnly after you grant location permission. (1) While you record a ride, the app saves your route (latitude, longitude, speed and time) so we can show distance, history and playback. On iPhone and Android the app keeps recording with the screen locked while a ride is in progress (the system shows its location indicator or notification). (2) While you are "live" on the crew map, your current position is shared with other signed-in riders. Going Ghost, closing the app or setting a privacy zone around home or work removes you from the map. Positions older than a few minutes disappear from the map.
Motion sensorsThe phone's accelerometer and gyroscope are used on your phone to calculate lean angle during a ride. The resulting lean numbers are saved with the ride. Raw sensor streams are not uploaded.
PhotosPhotos of your bike or rides that you choose to upload for your garage, posts or a service request. The app only sees photos you pick; it does not browse your library.
Posts and messagesFeed posts, comments, likes, direct messages to friends you accepted, group-ride (PackRide) messages and positions, hazard reports (anonymous, no account attached) and routes you save or import (GPX, TCX or KML files you choose).
Service requestsIf you ask the Lab for a bike service look: the issue, bike, urgency, an optional phone number, and the typed name on the service agreement. Visible to you and the Lab mechanics only.
Safety info (optional)Emergency contacts and medical notes you choose to enter. Stored in a private record only you can read. The SOS button builds a text message with your location and hands it to your phone's own messaging app. We do not send it.
TechnicalStandard server logs and an authentication token needed to keep you signed in, handled by Google Firebase.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in riders can see your name, handle, bike, city, ride summaries (miles, time, top speed, best lean), leaderboard rank, public garage and posts, and your live position while you are live. People you block cannot see your live position.
  • Only you can read your full ride routes, safety info and your service requests (plus Lab mechanics for service requests).
  • Nobody outside the app can browse accounts. Signing in is required.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account and app data on our behalf.
  • Apple and Google for Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in, if you choose them.
  • Photon geocoder (komoot.io) receives the place name you type into route search, with your approximate position to rank nearby results. Nothing else is sent.
  • The web version at app.riderlabkc.com also uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure site usage and OpenFreeMap for map tiles. The iPhone and Android apps do not include those analytics tools.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Live positions are overwritten continuously and drop off the map after a few minutes. When you delete your account, your profile, rides and routes, photos, posts, messages, positions, safety info and service requests are deleted, and so is your sign-in.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • Turn location or motion permission off at any time in your phone's Settings. Ride tracking and the live map need location.
  • Use Ghost mode to hide from the map and privacy zones to hide around home and work.
  • Delete your account in the app (You tab, delete account), or follow the steps at thestaleysocial.com/riderlab/delete-account, or email us from the address on your account.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data or a correction at the same email.

Children

Rider Lab KC is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and access is limited by account-based rules in the database. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your sign-in private.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and note it in the app.
