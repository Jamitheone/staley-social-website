Delete your Rider Lab KC account

You can delete your account and data yourself, any time.

In the app

  1. Open Rider Lab KC and go to the You tab.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email thestaleysocial@gmail.com from the email address on the account (or include your rider handle). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and handle, garage bikes and photos
  • All rides, saved routes and imported ride files
  • Posts, comments, likes, direct messages you sent, and group-ride messages
  • Your live position, privacy zones, emergency contacts and medical notes
  • Service requests and the signed agreement
  • Your sign-in (email, Apple or Google link)

Anonymous hazard reports carry no account link, so they stay up until they expire on their own. Deletion cannot be undone.
