Common questions

Where is my build saved?

On your device only. Open the Saved Builds tab to load or delete a build. Deleting the app deletes everything, and there is no cloud backup, so a new phone starts empty.

Why does the app say a point cost is unknown?

The cost of an attribute point is not something we can verify, so the app never guesses a price. It only shows what it can stand behind.

A gate threshold looks wrong after a game update.

Thresholds are fan-collected and can change with a game patch. Check the number in your game before you spend. Tell us what changed and we will update the app.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No. It works fully offline and asks for no permissions.
