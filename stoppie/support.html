Common questions

How do I play?

Hold the screen to pull a wheelie. Let go to drop onto the front wheel for a stoppie. Keep the bike inside the balance zone, switch between the two to build a streak multiplier, and push your best score.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. The game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no network code. It works offline.

Where is my best score kept?

On your phone only. Deleting the game deletes the score.

Does it have purchases?

Not in version 1.0. If an optional bike pack is added later, Apple handles the purchase and the game still collects nothing.
